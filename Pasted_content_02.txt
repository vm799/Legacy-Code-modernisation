The Mega-Prompt for Your Deck
Copy and paste this into your AI image/slide generator to get the "Architect-level" look and feel:

Use 'Cyber-Industrial' photography—abstract 3D renders of glass, light-paths, and modular structures. No cheesy stock photos of people in suits.
> Content Requirements:
>  * Slide 1-3 (The Problem): Highlight that 'Legacy' isn't just old code; it's lost documentation, brittle libraries, and tribal knowledge. Use the phrase 'The Velocity Killer.'

>  * Slide 7-10 (The Assembly Lines): Explain three distinct paths: 1. API Wrap (for stable but old cores), 2. Automated Refactor (for library/cloud-native updates), and 3. Clean-Sheet Rearchitect (for high-value strategic assets).
>  * Technical Call-outs: Include mentions of 'Abstracting the Data Layer,' 'Sidecar Patterns,' 'Automated Regression Suites,' and 'SRE Observability.'
>  * Slide 11-12 (The Value): Focus on 'Continuous Modernization'—shifting from a one-time project to a permanent capability. End with a roadmap for a 90-day Pilot Phase.
> Tone: Authoritative, visionary, and architecturally precise."
